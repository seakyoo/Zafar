## Vision

I think Flisk should be moving toward AI-native development and prioritize features that move in this direction.

## Next 6 months

| Item | Who it is for | Impact | Confidence | Ease | ICE | Number to watch |
|------|---------------|--------|------------|------|-----|-----------------|
| Basic performance stats such as response time | Developers and AI coding agents | 7 | 8 | 9 | 504 | % of running services with response-time metrics |
| Flexible services orchestration - allowing selective import of services into Flisk for large projects | Developers working with large, multi-service projects | 8 | 8 | 7 | 448 | Reduction in average startup time using selective services |
| Support for concurrent active workspaces and multiple AI agents sharing the same project | Developers and teams using multiple AI coding agents | 10 | 6 | 5 | 300 | % of parallel agent tasks completed successfully |

## Not now

Supporting additional runtimes comes with its own overheads. The currently supported runtimes are widely used, and at this time we should focus on making existing runtimes more capable. Additional runtimes can be considered once existing ones are mature.
